| D1–D6 | LED | 5 mm | 6 |

| R7–R8 | Resistor | 4.7 kΩ | 2 |
| R9–R10 | Resistor | 47 kΩ | 2 |
| Q1–Q2 | NPN | 2N3904 | 2 |
| C1–C2 | Electrolytic capacitor | 10 µF | 2 |
| LDR1 | Photoresistor | LDR | 1 |
| RV1 | Potentiometer | actual kit value | 1 |
| SW1 | Push button | 6 mm | 1 |
| BT1–BT2 | CR2032 holder | 3 V | 2 |
