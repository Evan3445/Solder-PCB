## BOM
NE555 timer (DIP-8) × 1
Red 5 mm LED × 6
470 Ω resistor × 6
47 kΩ resistor × 4
4.7 kΩ resistor × 1
Photoresistor (LDR) × 1
1 kΩ potentiometer × 1
2N3904 transistor × 2
10 µF capacitor × 2
6 mm push button × 1
CR2032 battery holder × 1
